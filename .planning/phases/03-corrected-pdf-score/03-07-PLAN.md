## Phase Goal

Original ROADMAP goal: A singer can upload a supported printed PDF, review the recognized single vocal part against the original, correct it, and confirm a trustworthy score version for practice.

Derived framing: **As a** singer, **I want to** upload, review and correct a supported printed PDF, **so that** I can confirm a trustworthy score version for practice.

<objective>
Expand the proven upload into durable observable recognition jobs that recover safely and preserve partial work.
Purpose: A singer can leave/reopen processing, cancel, retry and understand a partial or unsupported result without losing usable notation.
Output: Fenced SQLite job queue, bounded worker lifecycle and accurate status/error/cancel UI.
</objective>
