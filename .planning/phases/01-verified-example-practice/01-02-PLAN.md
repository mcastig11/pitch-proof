### Bounded pause/divergence behavior (planning recommendation, not user-approved)

The score clock stays anchored to the verified fixture tempo. A singer pause does not stop the score clock or infer a missed note; no-pitch status remains descriptive. Do not infer position divergence from silence, a wrong pitch, or octave ambiguity. On browser visibility loss, AudioContext suspension, or a measured timeline gap longer than one fixture beat, freeze the last confirmed marker, remove the current-position claim, and show Position uncertain / ungraded. Keep an “I lost my place” action available while Following so a singer can explicitly enter the same recovery state. Recovery selects a valid measure, clears stale provisional observations, and runs the same count-in scheduler; D-01, D-02, and D-03 apply unchanged. This recommendation bounds Phase 1 position tracking to an audio-clock timeline and does not claim a general singer-following algorithm.
