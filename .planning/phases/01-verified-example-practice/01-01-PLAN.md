Keep the one fixture and its pure validation/position-mapping functions in `practice-fixture.js` as separate exports, so the shared schema is tested directly without adding a second one-example data module. Keep capture, local sample analysis, click scheduling, and idempotent teardown in the pattern-mapped `practice-audio.js`; keep DOM/session orchestration in `script.js`.

### Planning recommendations (not user-locked decisions)

- Fixture choice and license: use a short original in-repository vocal exercise with one one-beat pickup, four full 4/4 measures, one explicit rest, one vocal part, and a fixed 96 BPM tempo. Keep the fixture data and its SVG notation authored in project source, record its provenance, and do not represent it as a third-party arrangement or make a separate public-domain/CC0 claim. This avoids importing a score whose edition rights would need separate verification.
- Singer pause and divergence: use the fixture's fixed AudioContext score clock. Silence during a written rest is expected; silence, a wrong pitch, or octave ambiguity never fabricates a note or changes score position. The score clock continues during singer silence. Do not add a pause/resume transport; Stop ends the unsaved attempt, and Retry starts a fresh count-in from the selected measure. If the browser suspends the audio clock or the timeline has a discontinuity, freeze the last confirmed position and expose measure-based recovery in Plan 02. These are bounded implementation recommendations, not approvals recorded in CONTEXT.md.

<threat_model>
Security target: OWASP ASVS L1; mitigate all high-severity threats. Microphone analysis remains local and in memory only, with no persistence or transmission.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Browser page to microphone device | Permission grants local live analysis. Audio is not recorded, persisted, routed to speakers, or transmitted. |
| Fixture data to DOM/timeline | Fixture fields influence labels, measure selection, notation, and timing; malformed values must not become markup or invented score content. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01-01 | Information Disclosure | Microphone session in script.js | high | mitigate | Keep input connected only to analyser; do not add MediaRecorder, storage, download, fetch, or speaker output; stop every track on teardown. |
| T-01-02 | Denial of Service | Async permission/session state | medium | mitigate | The tracer uses one explicit session generation and repeatable stop; Plan 02 completes denial/device/retry race coverage before exposing retry paths. |
| T-01-03 | Tampering | practice-fixture.js and DOM rendering | medium | mitigate | Validate required metadata/events, reject unknown measures, and render fixture-provided labels through text nodes/textContent. |
| T-01-04 | Denial of Service | Audio clock and animation lifecycle | medium | mitigate | Cancel scheduled sources and visual loops and close/disconnect audio resources on every terminal/error path. |
</threat_model>
